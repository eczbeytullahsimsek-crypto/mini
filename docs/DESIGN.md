## Brand & Style
The design system embodies a calm, tactile sanctuary for digital reflection, journaling, and freeform ideation. Designed for thinkers, writers, and visual creators, it strips away digital fatigue in favor of quiet intentionality and warm physical resonance. 

The aesthetic harmonizes Scandinavian minimalism with subtle tactile realism:
- **Atmosphere:** Serene, meditative, warm, and uncluttered. It honors the analog purity of thick cotton paper and archival ink while offering precise digital fluidity.
- **Physicality:** Subtle paper grain textures, translucent washes, hairline parchment dividers, and gentle layered depth emulate fine stationery and bound leather folios without leaning into skeuomorphic gimmickry.
- **Interaction Feel:** Deliberate, soft transitions, elastic scroll resistance, and subtle micro-haptics emulate real-world friction and deliberate craft.

## Colors
The palette evokes natural stationery, vegetable-dyed linen, and natural minerals:
- **Primary (`#6C8372` - Lichen Sage):** Grounded, restful organic green used for active creation tools, primary CTAs, active stroke states, and verified indicators.
- **Secondary (`#8A8199` - Heather Lavender):** Muted, introspective floral tone reserved for bookmarks, highlighted passages, tag badges, and creative collection markers.
- **Tertiary (`#C9B69C` - Warm Sandstone):** Sun-dried paper and canvas tone used for cover accents, ambient container tints, and natural metadata ribbons.
- **Neutral (`#7A7672` - Pebble Ink):** Balanced warm mineral tone anchoring high-legibility body copy and structural elements.
- **Canvas & Surface Base:** Cream and bone surfaces (`#FBF9F5` base background, `#F5F2EA` paper card surface, `#EDE9DE` sunken shelf containers). Never use pure digital white (`#FFFFFF`) or pure black (`#000000`). Text rests at deep charcoal ink (`#242321`).

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy balances literary grace with contemporary clarity:
- **Display & Headline Roles (Newsreader):** Brings an authentic editorial and bound-book spirit to notebook titles, chapter dividers, and journal entry dates. The italic variant should be selectively applied for quotes, reflections, and atmospheric section subtitles.
- **Body & Label Roles (Manrope):** Geometric yet friendly, providing unmatched legibility across tablet toolbars, sidebars, dense index view cards, and property sheets.
- **Rhythm & Line Length:** Reading canvases enforce a 65–75 character measure with generous line heights (`1.55` to `1.6`) to maintain cognitive ease during sustained writing and reading sessions.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout balances fluid canvas flexibility with structural shelf clarity:
- **Canvas Philosophy:** In document/sketch mode, the canvas adapts as an unbounded or paged tactile sheet surrounded by floating toolbars. In browse/shelf mode, a fluid responsive grid displays notebook binders and paper sheets.
- **Breakpoints & Grids:**
  - **Phone (<640px):** Single-column shelf layout with bottom tool sheets; full-width canvas viewport with collapsed floating pill menus.
  - **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 2 to 3-column notebook grid; split-pane view for notebook index and page thumbnail scrubber.
  - **Desktop / Landscape Tablet (>1024px):** Fixed left navigation rail (`280px`), expandable 4-column document gallery, or full side-by-side dual-page spread.
- **Rhythm:** Layout spaces strictly honor an 8px baseline cadence (with 4px sub-divisions for fine toolbar icon placement).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is expressed through tonal stacking, paper thickness, and warm diffused light:
- **Layer 0 (Desk / Canvas Base):** Deep cream ground (`#F5F2EA`).
- **Layer 1 (Paper Sheet / Card):** Light warm off-white surface (`#FBF9F5`) defined by a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(108, 98, 88, 0.08)`) and an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 2px 12px -2px rgba(92, 82, 70, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(92, 82, 70, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Palettes & Toolbars):** Frosted warm parchment glass (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.88)` with `16px` backdrop blur) coupled with an ambient drop: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(78, 68, 56, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(78, 68, 56, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Sheet Overlays):** Soft elevated panels cast a directional downward glow: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(60, 52, 42, 0.12)`. Avoid harsh black drop shadows; all shadows inherit the amber-brown hue of natural ambient light.

## Shapes
Forms communicate tactile comfort and organic softness:
- **Base Radii:** Standard interactive elements (buttons, inputs, tool cells) employ `rounded-lg` (0.5rem to 0.75rem). Notebook covers, canvas surfaces, and modal sheets use `rounded-2xl` (1rem to 1.5rem) to evoke rounded cardstock and bound leather corners.
- **Floating Controls:** Floating tool dock containers, stylus tool capsules, and tags leverage full continuous pill geometry (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular notebook pages.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Lichen Sage (`#6C8372`) with bone-white text (`#FAF8F5`). Soft corner smoothing (`0.75rem`), hover shift to `#5B7060`, and subtle scale compression (`0.98`) on tap/click.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Parchment tint (`#EFEBE1`) with deep ink text (`#242321`) and a hairline sand border (`rgba(108, 98, 88, 0.12)`).
- **Floating Action Tool Buttons:** Circular or pill-shaped translucent containers (`44px` minimum touch target) with centered vector icons rendered in warm charcoal.

### Floating Stylus Tool Dock
- A persistent, repositionable dock housing pen, pencil, highlighter, eraser, lasso, and color wells.
- Finished with frosted parchment glass (`rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.92)`), `rounded-full` contour, and a micro-separator between drawing tools and utility actions.
- Selected tool is enveloped by a soft sage indicator capsule with a subtle haptic bounce.

### Cards & Notebook Covers
- **Notebook Spines & Covers:** Ratio-proportioned cards resembling bound books. Covers carry solid or muted duotone palettes (sage, sand, lavender, terracota) featuring a subtle left-edge spine shadow gradient and centered serif typography.
- **Page Previews:** Off-white miniature sheets showing faint ruled, dotted, or blank textures with soft corner curves and low-contrast perimeter outlines.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) indicators. Background is a tinted pastel wash (e.g., Heather Lavender at 12% opacity: `rgba(138, 129, 153, 0.12)`) paired with high-contrast text (`#4D445B`). Padding: `0.25rem 0.75rem`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Recessed containers (`#EFEBE2`) with no harsh focus rings. Focus states activate a gentle Lichen Sage outer border ring (`1.5px solid #6C8372`) and a soft inner ambient glow. Placeholder text rests in muted warm sand (`#8F8A83`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Rounded squares (`6px` radius) for checkboxes; circular rings for radio buttons. Unchecked state uses a delicate pebble border (`1.5px solid #BCB6AC`). Checked state fills smoothly with Sage (`#6C8372`) displaying an off-white checkmark.

### Additional Specialty Components
- **Paper Template Selector:** Segmented control toggling between Blank, Dotted, College Ruled, Grid, and Cornell layouts.
- **Color Swatch Wells:** Circular physical-feel color pucks with subtle debossed rim rings indicating the currently loaded ink.
